How to play

Read the street signs and clues, then choose Left, Straight or Right. Use the compass heading to work out which way is which.

Scoring (max 1000): +100 per correct turn, +25 bonus if you needed no mistakes or hints there, −50 per wrong turn, hints cost −10 / −25 / −50 (levels 1 to 3). Speed does not affect your score.

Wrong turn? You’re sent back to the junction and that road is closed off. You never restart.

The town is fictional (“Maple Hollow”). It is a schematic practice map, not a real place.
